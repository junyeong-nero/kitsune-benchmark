# Kitsune main `514b5b7`: lite suite under the decline policy

[Results index](README.md) · [Results by Kitsune version](kitsune-versions.md) ·
[Previous: main `dea1ad4` under decline and accept](2026-11-01-main-dea1ad4-elicitation.md)

**Bottom line:**

- **Successes:** Kitsune main solves every lite unit again (306/306 scorable runs). Playwright misses one, crowded
  `mm-audio` (`no_attempt`).
- **Holds:** 27 Kitsune runs had a viewed-tab hold, one prompt each (`dea1ad4` decline: 1.3 per held run). Every hold
  was declined, and the agent redid the declined writes in another tab in all 27 runs. After kitsune#390 a declined
  hold means "not in this tab" and the hint says to use a tab of the agent's own (kitsune#383), so the redo is now the
  intended answer.
- **Input, Kitsune/Playwright, personal:** 1.385 on the 11 units with a hold against 0.841 on the 45 without. The
  held units still cost what they cost on `dea1ad4` under decline (1.425 on 12); the redo is the cost. Making the
  user's tab read-only for the agent is kitsune#393.
- **Input by track:** personal 0.934 (`dea1ad4` 0.943), crowded 0.627 (0.704), isolated 0.910 (0.859), coexistence
  user-seeded 0.992 (0.946), external 0.960 (1.165).
- **Coexistence:** unchanged. User view 2/28 Playwright vs 28/28 Kitsune; typing input 0/3 vs 3/3, 429 vs 0
  misdirected characters.

One attempt, no order counterbalancing; read differences as observations.

## Comparing with v0.7.1

v0.7.1 had no holds to answer: the viewed-tab hold arrived with kitsune#364 on main, after v0.7.1. Its suite also
recorded no elicitation policy. Since #73, `analyze-release.py` reads its Kitsune `git describe` and reports "no
holds then (Kitsune < #364)" for it instead of a not-comparable warning. The
v0.7.1 columns above are context; the personal and coexistence ratios include the redo cost that v0.7.1 never had.

## Limits

- One attempt, Kitsune and Playwright concurrent; elapsed times are under shared load.
- The first run and the retry ran about 1.5 hours apart, on the same revisions.
- Decline answers no to every prompt, which is not a real user.
- The live tasks in external are ungraded.
- No hostile-UI matrix was run on this revision.
